Dataspace Management · mockup · phase 1 of 3

Onboarding

A person without a company (Erika Mustermann, Hans Beispiel) registers their company at OSP A: they choose what they want to do and how they prove the company (Business Wallet or supporting documents), check the company data, pick the company roles, accept the terms and submit; afterwards they follow the review and may withdraw. OSP staff (Rolf Prüfer, Otto Normalverbraucher) see every application. Backend module c1-m2 Registration (specs/c1-m2/openapi.yaml); the use cases and the in-memory adapter already exist in packages/c1-m2-core, and shared/data.js equals its fixtures. Each view below is one screen; decide them one at a time, then build the proof of concept from the decided views.

1

Start application

decided

Erika Mustermann (applicant, no company) opens Onboarding. Step 1 asks what she wants to do, as cards: the cards stay visible: Register a new company shows step 2 right below them; My company is already in the dataspace first asks what happened to it: Reactivate it at OSP A (BPN required) or Move it from another OSP (which OSP, and the BPN if it was at OSP A before), and step 2 appears below once she chose. Step 2 asks how she proves the company. With the Business Wallet the wallet step follows and POST /applications creates the application; with documents she continues to the company data, and the application is created with them.

The screen, with its configurator

Data from the spec

  • POST /api/c1-m2/applications (createApplication, register_company, own): kind (new company → ONBOARDING, reactivate → REACTIVATION, move → TRANSFER), verification, attestation, bpn (reference to a company of OSP A: required for REACTIVATION, only for a return with TRANSFER), companyData, companyRoles.
  • "From which OSP?" (move): not in the spec; the options come from FRAMEWORK 7a (OSP B) in shared/data.js.
  • Results shown as states: 201 created / 200 repeated (continue), 409 open-application-exists with existingApplicationId, 403 account-bound, 400 documents-disabled, 502 upstream-error.
  • GET /api/c1-m2/applications (listApplications): an open application before offering a new one.

Open questions

  • Backend/BFF (P0): how does the browser get the verification attestation of the wallet step? There is no BFF endpoint yet (FRAMEWORK 5.2 step 2).
  • Backend (C1-M2): companyRoles are required at creation for ONBOARDING, so roles must be chosen before the wallet step, or sent as a default and changed in step 3 with PATCH. Make them optional at creation?
  • Backend (C1-M2): the UI cannot know before the choice whether the path DOCUMENTS is switched on (DOCUMENTS_ENABLED); it needs a flag, otherwise the person learns it only from 400 documents-disabled.
  • Backend (C1-M2): REACTIVATION and the return need a companyId or bpn; a person without a company cannot look it up. Is the BPN as free text acceptable?
  • Backend (C1-M2, C1-M3, C3-M2): the move case asks which OSP the company comes from, but ApplicationCreate has no field for it, and bpn only refers to a company of OSP A (a return). Should C1-M2 take the current OSP (and the BPN the company has at the CSP) for a TRANSFER, or does the C1-M3 pre-check find them at the CSP (README 6.2)? Which operation lists the OSPs to choose from?
  • Decide: check for an open application when the page opens (listApplications), or only react to 409 on create?

Decided 2026-11-06: progress=side · choice=tiles. "What do you want to do?" is the first step, as cards that stay visible; the proof appears below them (a new company at once, an existing one after choosing reactivation or move, with their fields).

2

Company data

decided

After the wallet step Erika sees what the Business Wallet confirmed and the data of her application, with the comparison per field; she may change the legal name, short name, address and her own identifiers (EBWOID, EUID and country stay the wallet's). Hans Beispiel (DOCUMENTS) enters everything here.

The screen, with its configurator

Data from the spec

  • GET /api/c1-m2/applications/{applicationId} (getApplication): companyData.legalName, shortName, address.*, identifiers[].type/value/source, comparison[].field/result (badge: FROM_ATTESTATION "From the wallet", MATCH/MISMATCH "Edited by you, matches/differs", NOT_IN_ATTESTATION "Entered by you, not in the wallet"; short name and identifiers with source APPLICANT "Entered by you"), attestation.person, representation.confirmed/role/validUntil, verifiedAt, expiresAt.
  • PATCH …/applications/{applicationId} (updateApplication): legalName, shortName, address, identifiers; 400 attestation-mismatch (fields), 400 validation (errors).
  • GET /countries, GET /countries/{countryCode}/identifier-types (pattern shown as format help); DOCUMENTS: POST /applications with companyData.

Open questions

  • Backend (C1-M2): the submission also fails 30 days after issuedAt (ATTESTATION_MAX_AGE), earlier than expiresAt may suggest. The page must not compute that date: needs a field such as submittableUntil.
  • Backend (C1-M2): EBWOID is not allowed in the path DOCUMENTS, but identifier-types lists it; the mockup leaves it out as a pre-check. Should the API say which types fit which path?
  • Backend (C1-M2): PATCH replaces the address as a whole, so after changing only the street, postal code and city also read "Edited by you" (MATCH), and the backend reports COUNTRY as MATCH although the country cannot be changed; the mockup keeps the country "From the wallet". Should unchanged parts keep FROM_ATTESTATION?
  • Decide: may the person change data the wallet proved (the backend allows it and records MISMATCH), or only fill in what is missing?

Decided 2026-11-06: comparison=badges · editing=button. The Edit button switches the read-only view to the form; Save and Cancel switch back; the badges say whether a value is from the wallet or edited by the person.

3

Supporting documents

decided

Hans Beispiel (path DOCUMENTS) uploads the commercial register extract of Beispiel Logistik GmbH and, if it does not name him, a power of attorney. OSP staff check them by hand.

The screen, with its configurator

Data from the spec

  • POST …/applications/{applicationId}/documents (uploadDocument, multipart file + documentType): DocumentInfo documentId, documentType, fileName, sizeBytes, sha256, uploadedAt; 400 validation, 409 invalid-state.
  • GET …/documents/{documentId} (getDocument), DELETE …/documents/{documentId} (deleteDocument).
  • SubmitRequest.representationDeclared (sent at submission).

Open questions

  • Backend (C1-M2): required document types, maximum size and count are configuration of the module; the page repeats them as text. An API with requiredDocumentTypes, maxBytes, maxCount?
  • Backend (C1-M2): the UI offers one document per type; the API allows several per type (up to DOCUMENT_MAX_COUNT). Is one per type enough for the review (e.g. a register extract of several pages as one PDF)?

Decided 2026-11-06: declaration=here; instead of the upload and list alternatives: one expandable section per document type (commercial register extract, power of attorney, other); without a document it shows the upload area, after the upload the document with Open and Delete. One document per type in the UI (the API allows several).

4

Roles, terms and submission

decided

Erika picks the company roles; the page lists the terms GET /agreements returns for them (the page does not compute which terms apply). Each document has an Open button: a full-screen viewer shows the PDF, with a language selector top right and Cancel / Accept at the bottom; Accept is enabled only after she scrolled to the end, in the language shown. She submits when every document is accepted.

The screen, with its configurator

Data from the spec

  • GET /company-roles (role, name, description, agreementKeys); roles changed with PATCH … companyRoles.
  • GET /agreements?companyRole=… (agreementKey, version, party, title, documentUrls.de/en: the viewer's languages are the keys of documentUrls; security-regulations is English only).
  • Accept in the viewer → ConsentInput agreementKey, version, language (the language shown when the person reached the end; switching the language starts the reading over).
  • POST …/submit (submitApplication): consents[].agreementKey/version/language, representationDeclared; 400 consents-incomplete (missing), 400 documents-incomplete, 409 attestation-expired; then PUT …/attestation (replaceAttestation).

Open questions

  • Phase 2 / backend: "scrolled to the end" cannot be detected on a PDF shown by the browser's own viewer (and not across origins). Either the portal renders the PDF itself (pdf.js: a new dependency, Eclipse Dash IP check, and CORS on the document hosts), or C1-M2 offers the terms as text/HTML next to documentUrls. Which one?
  • Decide: roles here or at the start (see the open question of view 1).

Decided 2026-11-06: summary=top · roles=checkboxes. Terms: one row per document with its icon and an Open button; Open shows a full-screen viewer with the language selector top right and Cancel / Accept at the bottom; Accept only after scrolling to the end.

5

My application

decided

Erika opens Onboarding again after she started: the page shows the status and review status of her application, what she sent, and lets her withdraw until OSP staff decide. Hans Beispiel sees why his earlier application was declined.

The screen, with its configurator

Data from the spec

  • GET /applications (listApplications) to find it; GET /applications/{applicationId}: status, reviewStatus, declineReason, createdAt, submittedAt, withdrawnAt, closedAt, completedAt, consents[].givenAt.
  • POST …/withdraw (withdrawApplication): 200, 409 already-decided (reviewStatus), 409 invalid-state.

Open questions

  • Backend (C1-M2, P1): flags canWithdraw (and canContinue): the page must not derive them from status and reviewStatus; the mockup shows them from a field that is not in the spec.
  • Backend (P1): a live update when reviewStatus or status changes (SSE); C1-M2 has no stream for the applicant.
  • BFF/C2-M5: after COMPLETED the person is Company Admin; how does the session (/me, companyId) learn it without signing in again?

Decided 2026-11-06: withdraw=bottom · status=stepper · details=tabs. The horizontal stepper has the icons of the timeline.

6

Applications

decided

Rolf Prüfer (OSP Reviewer) and Otto Normalverbraucher (OSP Admin) see every application of OSP A, filter it, open one and its documents. The decision itself is made in the review case of C1-M3 Application Review.

The screen, with its configurator

Data from the spec

  • GET /osp/applications (listAllApplications, view_applications, osp): filters status, kind, verification, companyId, page, size; ApplicationSummary fields.
  • GET /osp/applications/{applicationId} (getAnyApplication), GET …/documents/{documentId} (getAnyDocument); 403 forbidden without the permission.

Open questions

  • Decide: does this list belong to Onboarding, or to the review feature on C1-M3 (review cases), so that staff have one list?
  • Backend (C1-M2): no search by company name in listAllApplications; the mockup offers only the filters of the API.
  • Backend (C1-M2/C1-M3): the application carries no link to its review case (reviewCaseId).

Decided 2026-11-06: filters=side · layout=table. Filter and list in one card, the count in the filter row; an opened application replaces them in the same card, with a back button top left.
