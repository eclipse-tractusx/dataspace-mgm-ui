Dataspace Management · mockup · phase 1 of 3

Wallets as a service

Every company of this onboarding service provider (OSP A) gets a dataspace wallet during onboarding, and in it two credentials: the BPN credential and the membership credential. Its connectors sign in to the wallet with the wallet accounts of technical users. People of the company see the wallet, the credentials and the wallet accounts, and retrieve the access data of a wallet account once; the staff of the OSP watch the credentials of all companies and confirm a revocation done by hand. Behind it are two backend modules: c4-m2 Dataspace Wallet Interface and c4-m1 Credentials (portal-backend-spec). Each view below is one screen of the portal with a configurator: switch the alternatives on the screen, copy the combination you like, and it becomes the decision of the view.

1

Your dataspace wallet

decided

Erika Mustermann, Company Admin of Beispiel Maschinenbau GmbH, opens Wallets as a service from the rail to check that the dataspace wallet of her company is ready, and to copy the DID and the addresses an IT colleague needs to set up a connector. It is the landing page of the feature for people of a company and links to the views 2 and 3. The navigation inside the feature (tabs or not) is decided here and applies to the views 2 and 3 too.

The screen shows the wallet like a phone wallet: the dataspace wallet and its two credentials are passes. Four layouts to compare: a stack of passes (select a pass to bring it to the front, its details beside it), one pass to turn over (front: identity and credentials; back: the connector set-up), tiles (a bento grid with one tile per topic) and the classic cards by topic. The card style (carbon, Eclipse Tractus-X orange, frosted glass) applies to every layout.

The screen, with its configurator

Data from the spec

  • GET /api/c4-m2/companies/{companyId}/wallet (getWallet, view_dataspace_wallet, scope company) → Wallet: provider (CSP), status (REQUESTED, VALIDATING, ACTIVE, FAILED), did, didDocumentLocation, credentialServiceUrl, bdrsUrl, stsTokenUrl (IdentityHub only), supportsWalletAccounts, failureReason, createdAt, activatedAt.
  • The credential passes and tiles also read GET /api/c4-m1/companies/{companyId}/credentials (view 2). The number of wallet accounts comes from the C2-M3 list (view 3).
  • The company name and BPN in the header come from C3-M1 GET /companies/{companyId}; Wallet has neither.
  • States: validating and failed (failureReason DID_NOT_RESOLVABLE) are results of the same call; no-wallet = 404 urn:c4-m2:problem:not-found (no row yet); forbidden = 403 for Max (Member).

Open questions

  • Card style: one style for every company, or a style per OSP from the branding of GET /config?
  • C4-M2: Wallet returns no bpn, although the table has it. Add it, so the page needs no second call to C3-M1 for the header?
  • C2-M5: view_dataspace_wallet is a proposal for IT Admin and Company Admin (6.8). Should a Member see the DID and the addresses (they are public anyway)?
  • Live updates: VALIDATING becomes ACTIVE in the background (6.10). Which SSE event does the UI subscribe to (DataspaceWalletCreated is an outbox event for modules)?

Decided 2026-11-06: layout=stack · skin=carbon · values=full · nav=tabs. Change asked: the credentials stand on the right as their own stack of passes (the wallet pass, the connector set-up and the wallet accounts on the left), and every credential pass has a slot for the logo of its dataspace (Catena-X next to, for example, a semiconductor dataspace; state two-dataspaces). The wallet pass keeps the QR code of the DID (decided 2026-11-06; drawn with qrcode.react behind QrCode in libs/shared/ui-mui).

2

Your credentials

decided

Erika comes here from the wallet page, or after a notification, to see whether the BPN credential and the membership credential of her company are issued: without them, the connectors of other participants do not exchange data with hers. The page only reads; the company cannot request or revoke a credential in version 0.2.

The screen, with its configurator

Data from the spec

  • GET /api/c4-m1/companies/{companyId}/credentials (listCompanyCredentials, view_credentials, scope company) → CredentialStatusView[]: credentialType (BPN, MEMBERSHIP), status (NONE, REQUESTED, ISSUED, FAILED, CANCELLED, REVOCATION_PENDING, REVOKED), requestedAt, issuedAt, revokedAt. No messages of the issuer, no route (7.1).
  • The states are the results of the same call: issued, being-issued (membership REQUESTED), failed, revoked (company deactivated, membership REVOKED, BPN stays), not-requested (both NONE); forbidden = 403 for Max.
  • The texts that explain a credential ("Proves the business partner number…") are UI texts (i18n), not from the API.
  • The layout "Passes" draws each credential as a pass of the phone wallet, like the passes of view 1 (BPN credential carbon, membership credential orange).

Open questions

  • C4-M1: the "Overall status" banner is computed in the UI from the list, which is a decision the UI should not take. Can listCompanyCredentials return the last round (run.status RUNNING, DONE, FAILED), as the internal query 9.1 does? Without it the banner drops.
  • C4-M1: a credential does not say which dataspace it belongs to. The logo slot of a pass needs dataspace (id, name, a logo address) per credential; today the UI could only take dataspace.memberOf of the installation, which works for one dataspace only.
  • C4-M1: CredentialStatusView has no revocationReason, so a revoked credential cannot say why (company deactivated or other).
  • C4-M1: FAILED without any hint for the company. Is a short, safe reason category for the company wanted (for example "being repeated", "your OSP was informed"), or is the fixed sentence enough?
  • Live updates: REQUESTED becomes ISSUED by a status report. Which SSE event does the page listen to, so it does not poll?

Decided 2026-11-06: layout=passes · explain=info · summary=none. Each pass carries the logo slot of its dataspace.

3

Wallet accounts

decided

Erika (or the IT Admin who created the technical user) comes here after creating a technical user with wallet access in Technical users (C2-M3), to retrieve the access data of its wallet account and enter them into the connector. They are handed out exactly once (C4-M2 6.7); whoever loses them deletes the technical user and creates it again.

The screen, with its configurator

Data from the spec

  • The technical users come from C2-M3 GET /companies/{companyId}/technical-users → TechnicalUserSummary (name, description, iamClientId, walletAccess); only those with walletAccess other than NONE are listed.
  • Per technical user GET /api/c4-m2/technical-users/{technicalUserId}/wallet-account (getWalletAccount, view_dataspace_wallet) → WalletAccount: walletAccountRef, provider, status, credentialsState (NONE, AVAILABLE, RETRIEVED, EXPIRED), credentialsExpireAt, createdAt.
  • POST …/wallet-account/credentials (retrieveWalletAccountCredentials, retrieve_wallet_credentials) → WalletAccountCredentials: tokenUrl, clientId, clientSecret, did, credentialServiceUrl; Cache-Control: no-store. The button shows from the permission of GET /me and credentialsState AVAILABLE.
  • Errors shown: 410 urn:c4-m2:problem:already-retrieved (state retrieved-meanwhile, someone else was faster); 410 credentials-expired, 409 no-credentials and 409 invalid-state are covered by credentialsState and the hidden button.
  • "Download as a file" builds the file in the browser from the response; nothing is stored. The secret in data.js is a mockup value.

Open questions

  • C4-M2: there is no list of the wallet accounts of a company; the page needs the C2-M3 list and one call per technical user. Add GET /companies/{companyId}/wallet-accounts?
  • Where does this screen live: here in Wallets as a service, in Technical users (C2-M3, right after creating the user), or in both? C2-M3 returns walletAccountRef on the technical user, so its detail page could offer the retrieval.
  • Security: is a download of the client secret as a file acceptable, or only copy (the file stays in the download folder)?
  • C4-M2: may a person retrieve the access data of a technical user that another person created? The spec allows every person with retrieve_wallet_credentials of the company.

Decided 2026-11-06: layout=detail · reveal=inline · confirm=ask.

4

Credentials of all companies

decided

Otto Normalverbraucher, OSP Admin, opens it from Wallets as a service to find credentials that need him: failed issuances, orders without a status report for more than 24 hours, and membership credentials of deactivated companies that he has to revoke at the Credential Issuer by hand. It is the landing page of the feature for OSP staff.

The screen, with its configurator

Data from the spec

  • GET /api/c4-m1/credentials (listCredentials, manage_credentials, scope osp) → CredentialPage (items, page, size, total) of CredentialDetail: id, companyId, bpn, credentialType, status, route, issuerDid, walletProvider, holderDid, requestRef, attempt, requestedAt, issuedAt, failedAt, revokedAt, failureReason, revocationReason, changedAt; sorted by changedAt descending.
  • Filters of the API: status (repeatable), credentialType, companyId, route, requestedBefore. "Open for more than 24 h" = status=REQUESTED&requestedBefore=now−24h. "Needs attention" = three queries: FAILED, REVOCATION_PENDING, open orders.
  • "Name and BPN" looks the names up at C3-M1 (listCompanies); the C4-M2 test companies have no name and show their BPN.
  • States: no-results (empty page); forbidden = 403 for Rolf (OSP Reviewer without manage_credentials).

Open questions

  • C4-M1: no counts per status; the status chips show counts only from the page in hand. Add counts (for example GET /credentials/summary) or drop them?
  • C4-M1: the list filters by companyId, not by BPN or name. Add a bpn filter (the column exists), or does the UI look up the company at C3-M1 first?
  • C4-M1: the 24 hours of "open" are RESEND_AFTER_HOURS of the installation; the UI must not hard-code them. Return the value (for example in GET /config) or a flag overdue per credential?
  • C4-M1: CredentialDetail has no company name; every row needs C3-M1. Add companyName?
  • C4-M1: can OSP staff start a new attempt for a failed credential here, or is that only Process Orchestration (OP-18)? Today the screen only reads.

Decided 2026-11-06: layout=attention · filters=chips · detail=page · company=name.

5

Credential

decided

Otto opens one credential from view 4 to see everything the issuer reported, and, for a revocation pending (route Credential Issuer cannot revoke, C4-M1 6.3), to confirm with a note that he revoked it at the Credential Issuer. The mockup links four example credentials (pending, failed, being issued, issued) above the title.

The screen, with its configurator

Data from the spec

  • One CredentialDetail (fields as in view 4).
  • POST /api/c4-m1/credentials/{credentialId}/confirm-revocation (confirmRevocation, manage_credentials, scope osp) with { note } (1–500 characters, no names) → 200 CredentialDetail with status REVOKED. Errors: 400 urn:c4-m1:problem:validation (empty note), 409 urn:c4-m1:problem:invalid-state (state conflict), 404 not-found.
  • The life cycle is built from requestedAt, failedAt, issuedAt, revokedAt and changedAt; earlier attempts are not visible.

Open questions

  • C4-M1: there is no GET /credentials/{credentialId}. A direct link (from a notification) cannot load one credential; add it?
  • C4-M1: the table credential_history (every status change with attempt and note) is not in the API. Return it in the detail, so the timeline shows all attempts and the note of the confirmation?
  • C4-M1: the UI shows "Confirm revocation" because status is REVOCATION_PENDING. Is that enough (the API enforces it with 409), or should CredentialDetail carry allowedActions?
  • C2-M2 / audit: the note may not hold names. Who did the confirmation is only in the audit log (sub); should the detail show it?

Decided 2026-11-06: layout=sections · confirm=dialog · history=timeline.

Not in this mockup

  • Dataspace wallets of all companies for OSP staff. "Wallets as a service" suggests that the OSP sees the wallets it provides, but C4-M2 has no operation with scope osp for people (only /internal queries for modules). Open question to C4-M2: add GET /wallets (status, provider, failure reason per company), so OSP staff find failed wallets? Then it becomes view 6.
  • Own wallet (bring your own, control proof). POST /wallet-control-challenges and …/proof are "later" (OP-29) and part of onboarding (applicant), not of this feature.
  • Requesting further credentials (use case credentials, certificates): Outlook of C4-M1, not in version 0.2.
